Where science becomes medicine.

Before a bottle is filled, the solution is prepared: ingredients are weighed, dissolved in purified water and mixed under controlled conditions, then sampled and released for filling.

  1. Weigh each ingredient dispensary
  2. Dissolve in purified water vessel
  3. Mix to a uniform solution agitator
  4. Sample & release QC
  5. Transfer to the filler line
